# Wattkraft Iberia

Photovoltaic components and integrated solutions from a single source.

Wattkraft Iberia is the Spanish and Portuguese branch of **Wattkraft**, the B2B photovoltaic distributor and system integrator founded in 2012 in Germany. From Madrid we work with distribution partners and on projects across the Iberian Peninsula.

### What we do

- Commercial and industrial self-consumption with battery storage
- EV charging projects in Spain and Portugal
- Technical studies and training for our partners
- Commissioning of inverters, transformer stations and battery systems
- After-sales service: hotline, remote support, investigation of complex incidents and warranty management
- Our own energy management system and zero-injection control

We develop our energy management and plant monitoring software here. Our repositories are private.
